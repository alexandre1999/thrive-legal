Thrive is made by Dineri (Alexandre Martins). You can delete your Thrive account and its data at any time.

## In the app

Open **Profile → Settings → Delete account** and confirm. The deletion happens straight away.

## Without the app

Email **management@dineri.info** from the address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my Thrive account"**. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you signed up with Apple's "Hide my email", write from that Apple account, or tell us the relay address.

## What is deleted

It is a real deletion, not a deactivation: your profile, messages, matches, photos, voice recordings, reflections, scores and game history are all removed.

## What is kept

Some things necessarily survive in a form that is no longer about you: a message you sent stays in the other person's conversation, and a photo added to an event's shared roll stays with that event. A subscription is billed by Apple or Google, so cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; deleting the account does not cancel it.

See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for everything we hold and how long we keep it.
